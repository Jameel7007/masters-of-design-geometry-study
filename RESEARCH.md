# Sufi Enneagram / Eleven Naqshbandi Principles

## Research architecture

This project places two distinct traditional structures into conversation:

1. **The Sufi Enneagram** as presented by Laleh Bakhtiar.
2. **The Eleven Rules / Principles of the Naqshbandiyya** as presented by Idries Shah and the supplied expanded Naqshbandi text.

The proposed one-principle/one-interaction overlay is a third layer: **our contemporary interpretive design**. No reviewed source assigns the eleven principles one-to-one to the enneagram's nine positions.

## Sources and limits

### A. Laleh Bakhtiar

Primary source: Laleh Bakhtiar, *The Sufi Enneagram: The Secrets of the Symbol Unveiled* (2013), checked directly against the supplied PDF.

Relevant printed pages (PDF page numbers in parentheses):

- **p. 14 (PDF 62):** macrocosm / microcosm; outward and inward movement; movement toward the center and away toward the circumference.
- **pp. 28-30 (PDF 76-78):** breath as rhythm in time; breath-spirit as movement; the line of spirit / movement / change; spatial and architectural continuity.
- **pp. 55-56 (PDF 103-104):** the recurring 1/7 decimal; 1, 4, 2, 8, 5, 7; 3, 6, 9; zero; the line of change / movement / spirit.
- **pp. 63-65 (PDF 111-113):** center and circumference; zero; the spiritual-heart triangle; three 120-degree segments; nine 40-degree divisions.

### B. Eleven Naqshbandi principles

Primary textual basis for the complete eleven-item order: Idries Shah, *A Perfumed Scorpion*, “The Eleven Rules of the Naqshbandiyya (Masters of the Design),” pp. 85-87 in the supplied excerpt / scanned edition.

Second source: the supplied “Principles of the Naqshbandi Way,” also published by the Naqshbandi Haqqani Sufi Order. The supplied text gives expanded entries for the first eight principles and includes attributions to Abdul Khaliq al-Ghujdawani, Ubaidullah al-Ahrar, Shah Naqshband, and Imam ar-Rabbani Ahmad al-Faruqi. It does **not** supply expanded entries for the final three *wuquf* principles.

Prescriptive passages in that second source - including breath retention, bodily visualization, and repetition counts - are evidence about a practice tradition, not material for this public experience. They will not be reproduced as instructions.

## Bakhtiar-supported geometry

The prototype uses only these settled geometric relationships:

| Element | Mathematical construction | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| Center | One point at `(cx, cy)`, labeled zero | **Directly supported** by Bakhtiar, pp. 56, 63-64 |
| Circumference | Circle of radius `r` around the center | **Directly supported**, pp. 63-64 |
| Nine positions | Nine equal angular intervals: `360 / 9 = 40 degrees` | **Directly supported**, p. 65 |
| Threefold structure | Three equal regions: `360 / 3 = 120 degrees` | **Directly supported**, p. 65 |
| 3 / 6 / 9 triangle | Connections `9 -> 3 -> 6 -> 9` | **Directly supported**, pp. 55-56, 65 |
| Movement line | Connections `1 -> 4 -> 2 -> 8 -> 5 -> 7 -> 1` | **Directly supported**, pp. 55-56 |
| Recurring decimal | `1 / 7 = 0.142857...` supplies the six-number order | **Directly supported**, pp. 55-56 |
| Spiritual-heart triangle | A smaller equilateral triangle clustered around the center | **Directly supported**, pp. 63-65 |

The prototype deliberately distinguishes the **outer 9/3/6 triangle used to verify the numeric graph** from Bakhtiar's **smaller spiritual-heart triangle near zero**. The future experience may show both, but it must not collapse them into a single unexplained shape.

## Terminology differences to preserve

- **Hosh / Hush dar dam:** transliteration varies. The supplied expanded source uses *Hosh*; the initial design prompt also uses *Hush*.
- **Baz gasht:** Shah gives **Restraint / pulling back**; the expanded source gives **Returning**. The visual concept may embody both, but copy must retain the difference.
- **Nigah dasht:** Shah gives **Watchfulness**; the expanded source gives **Attentiveness**.
- **Yad dasht / yada dasht:** Shah gives **Recollection / noting**; the expanded source spells *yada dasht* and emphasizes continuity.
- **Wuquf:** Shah renders the final three as **Pause**. The supplied expanded excerpt does not provide alternative entries for them.

## Practice safeguard

Shah states that practical execution belongs to personal tuition and careful adjustment. The experience will therefore contain no breath-holding method, prescribed dhikr count, guided visualization, initiation, promise of attainment, or imitation of teacher-directed exercises.

## Prototype conclusion

The geometry is sufficiently settled for a minimal engine:

- coordinates can be generated from one center, one radius, and equal angular division;
- the 9/3/6 and 1/4/2/8/5/7 graphs can be derived from number arrays;
- zero and the spiritual-heart triangle can remain separately addressable layers;
- no eleven-scene animation is needed to validate these relationships.

## Open questions before production

1. Decide the final title only after testing whether the project feels more like a sequence of attentional states (**The Eleven Watches**) or a geometric/spiritual lineage study (**Masters of the Design**).
2. Verify Persian-script spellings with a qualified source before publishing any script; transliteration alone is sufficient for the prototype.
3. Decide whether the final experience should show the large numeric 9/3/6 triangle, the smaller spiritual-heart triangle, or both at different moments.
4. Keep sound outside the core system until the silent motion language is proven.
